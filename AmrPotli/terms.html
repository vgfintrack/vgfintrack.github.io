Last updated: 23 October 2026

These are the rules for using AmrPotli (আমার পোটলি). By using the app you agree to them. They are written in plain words; if anything is unclear, write to us.

1. What AmrPotli is

  • AmrPotli is a notebook for your money: you write down what comes in, what goes out, what you owe and what is owed to you.
  • It is not a bank, a wallet or a payment app. It cannot send, receive or hold money, and it never connects to your bank, bKash, Nagad, Rocket or card.
  • It does not give financial, tax or legal advice. Totals and reports are worked out from what you type in, so please check numbers that matter before acting on them.

2. Your data is yours

  • Your records are saved in a private folder in your own Google Drive and in your browser. We do not have a copy and cannot see them. The privacy page explains this in full.
  • Because we cannot see your data, we cannot recover it for you. Download a backup from time to time (Setup → Backup & data); the app reminds you.
  • You are responsible for your Google account and your phone. If you share them, others may see your records. Turn on the app lock (PIN or fingerprint) for extra safety.
  • In a team shop, the owner chooses the helpers. Everyone the owner adds can see and add entries in the shop book, which is a file in the owner's Google Drive. The owner can remove a helper at any time.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Setup → Delete my account.

3. Free plan and paid plans

  • Today AmrPotli has a free plan with some limits (for example, up to 24 categories in total and 15 product groups for a shop).
  • In future we may offer paid plans with more features. Limits, features and prices may change; we will tell you in the app before a change affects you.
  • You will never be charged without choosing a paid plan yourself. If you stop paying, you keep your data; it stays in your Google Drive.

4. Information in future paid plans

To run a paid plan (to know who has paid, and to make the app better), we may need to keep a small amount of information about your account, such as:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • which plan you are on, when it started and when it ends;
  • the payment receipt for your plan (the amount paid for the plan, the date, and the payment reference number);
  • simple counts of how the app is used (for example, how many entries or which features are used), without amounts or names;
  • your phone or browser type, and error reports, to fix problems.

We will tell you clearly before any of this starts, and list exactly what is kept on the privacy page.

🔒 What we will never collect or ask for
  • Your bank account numbers, card numbers or bank login.
  • Your bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay account PIN, password or OTP code.
  • Your transactions, balances, loans or the notes and receipt photos you save in AmrPotli. These stay in your own Google Drive, as today.
  • Your contacts, messages or location.

Anyone who asks for your PIN or OTP in AmrPotli's name is a scammer. We will never call or message you for it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads based on your money.

5. Using the app fairly

  • Use AmrPotli for your own or your business's honest records, and follow the law.
  • Do not try to break, copy or misuse the app, or use it to harm others.
  • Customer and supplier names and phone numbers you add are your responsibility. Only add people who would be happy for you to keep their number, and only send them reminders they would expect.

6. No guarantee

We work hard to keep AmrPotli correct and available, but it is provided "as it is". It may sometimes have mistakes or be unavailable (for example, if Google sign-in or Google Drive is down). As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that come from relying on the app, from data lost on your devices or Google account, or from things outside our control.
