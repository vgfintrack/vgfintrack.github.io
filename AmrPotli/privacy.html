Privacy

AmrPotli (আমার পোটলি, "my money pouch") is a personal finance app that runs entirely in your browser.

Where your data is stored

Your accounts, transactions, budgets, settings and receipt photos are saved in two places only:

  • Your own Google Drive, in a hidden "app data" folder that only this app can open. It does not appear in your Drive file list and other apps cannot read it.
  • Your browser on the device you use, so the app opens quickly and works offline. Signing out removes this copy.

Potli tips (the “Potli says” card) and the “How do I…?” help are worked out on your phone from your own entries. They use no AI service and send nothing anywhere.

You can answer the first setup questions before signing in. Those answers stay only in your browser until you sign in with Google, and then move into your Drive. The app itself opens only after you sign in.

There is no server or database of ours. Your data is never sent to the people who run this website, and we cannot see it.

What the app asks Google for

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show who is signed in.
  • Access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive, to save and load your data. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • Only for team shops (and when you sign in with “I already use AmrPotli”): access to the Drive files AmrPotli made or that you chose with it. The app uses it for the shop book only. It still cannot see your other Drive files.

Team shops

When a shop owner adds helpers in Setup → Team, AmrPotli makes a “shop book” file in the owner's Google Drive and Google shares it with only the Gmail addresses the owner adds. Everyone in that team can see and add entries in the book, and each entry notes the email of who added it. The owner can remove a helper at any time, which ends their access. Helpers can open the shared file in their own Drive, so hiding totals from helpers in the app is not strict secrecy. Receipt photos stay in the Drive of the person who took them. The shop book never goes to the AmrPotli team; if the owner deletes their account, the shop book is deleted too.

What the app shares with the AmrPotli team

The app never sends your accounts, transactions, balances, notes or photos to us. It only does these things:

  • Reads two public lists: a short notice from the team (shown under the top bar), and the list of people the team has given more categories. That list holds only scrambled (hashed) emails; your phone checks your own email against it, so your email is not sent.
  • One anonymous count a day: that AmrPotli was opened that day, the language, whether it is in shop or simple mode, whether you are signed in and use the app lock, your kind of work as chosen from the app's list (for example Student or Pharmacy; if you typed your own, only “own profession”), and which features were used (for example voice, SMS paste, receipt photos), each as a simple +1. No name, email, device ID or amounts.
  • Once a week and once a month: +1 that AmrPotli was used that week or month (so the team can count weekly and monthly users), the country worked out from your phone's time zone (for example Bangladesh or Saudi Arabia, never a city or GPS location), and your district only if you chose one in the app. No name, email, device ID or amounts. You can turn all of these counts off in Setup → Help improve AmrPotli.
  • Only if you choose to send them: feedback you write in Send feedback, and the name, email, phone and promo code you type in Stay in touch. The team sees these and may reply. Ask us any time to remove them.

These are stored with Google Firebase. Like any website, Google's servers see the internet address a request comes from; we do not keep it.

Receipt photos

When you add a receipt or memo photo, the app makes it smaller on your phone and saves it in the same private Google Drive folder as your data (and in your browser, so it opens quickly). Nobody else can see it. Photos are not included in the backup file. When you delete an entry, its photo is removed from Drive a few days later.

SMS

AmrPotli never reads your messages by itself. It reads only an SMS you give it: one you share to AmrPotli (Android's Share menu), or copy and paste. If you allowed AmrPotli to paste (your phone asks the first time you tap Paste SMS), it looks at what you last copied when you open the app, and offers to add it only if it is a money SMS. The message is read on your phone and is not sent anywhere; only the entry you save is kept, in your Drive.

Voice entry

When you tap 🎤 Speak, your browser's own speech service turns your words into text (in Chrome this is done by Google). AmrPotli receives only the text, fills in the form for you to check, and keeps no recording. Nothing is heard unless you tap the button.

Feedback

Only what you type into Send feedback is sent, with the app version, language and where you opened it from. Nothing from your accounts or transactions is attached. Please don't write amounts, balances or account numbers in it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Setup → Delete my account. It deletes the AmrPotli file from your Google Drive and everything AmrPotli saved on that device (your data, sign-in and app lock), and removes the app's access to your Google account. It cannot be undone, so it offers a backup download first.

You can also do it without the app: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find AmrPotli, and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Future paid plans

AmrPotli may offer paid plans in future. Running them may need a little account information (such as your name, email, plan and payment receipt), and we will say exactly what before it starts. We will never collect your bank account or card details, your bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay PIN or OTP, or your transactions and balances. See the terms of use.
